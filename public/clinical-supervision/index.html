The Problem

Zero Tolerance for Missing Records

Clinical staff were required to complete monthly supervision documentation for child welfare cases — a regulatory requirement with zero tolerance for missing records. The process required data entry by clinical staff, sequential approvals from Clinical Directors and up to 10 concurrent supervisors, PDF dossier generation, and archival. Everything was done manually via email and physical forms, creating compliance risk and consuming significant staff time.

A New Leaf — Behavioral Health Division  ·  Timeline: 2022–2023 (maintained through 2026)  ·  Compliance: State behavioral health & child welfare regulations
The Solution

Concurrent Approvals, Auto-Generated Dossiers

Advanced JSON body and header formatting created highly responsive, visually clear interfaces for clinical staff — eliminating the need for a full Canvas App for routine data entry while significantly improving performance.

Microsoft Lists Architecture & Power Apps Forms
  • Clinical Supervision Sessions List — 30+ columns tracking session date, supervisor, supervisee, focus area, hours, notes. Custom Power Apps form with conditional visibility per supervision type.
  • Supervisees Registry List — certification requirements and compliance status, with auto-calculated compliance percentage.
  • Supervision Plans List — individual plans per clinician with auto-population from supervisee profile.
  • Audit Evidence List — audit artifacts with document links, approval signatures, and regulatory category codes.
Automation Architecture
  • Power Automate launched a multi-node Teams approval matrix routing to the supervising Clinical Director.
  • Concurrent approval branches sent simultaneous requests to up to 10 supervisors, computing approval quorum before proceeding.
  • Upon full approval, an automated pipeline generated a structured e-signed PDF dossier merging all session data and approval timestamps.
  • PDF was encrypted, watermarked, and automatically archived to the SharePoint Compliance Library with regulatory metadata tags.
