Enterprise SharePoint Vendor Lifecycle
& Contract Management System

A New Leaf, Mesa, AZ  ·  Timeline: 2022–2023 (maintained through 2026)  ·  Scale: 380+ active vendors, 5 departments, 2,300+ folder structures
Business Problem

The organization managed 380+ vendor and contractor relationships across five departments — Procurement, Facilities, HR, Legal, and Administration — through disconnected email chains, spreadsheets, and physical folders. Contract expiration dates were tracked manually in Excel, causing missed renewals and compliance risk. Approval routing was inconsistent and there was no central visibility into SLA compliance or vendor status.

SharePoint Architecture
  • Vendors Master List — core vendor registry with 40+ columns. Custom Power Apps form enforced conditional validation and multi-step data entry.
  • Contracts List — linked via lookup column. JSON column formatting applied color-coded SLA expiration indicators (green/yellow/red) visible directly in list view.
  • Vendor Services List — many-to-many relationship tracking individual services with pricing, SLA targets, and performance notes.
  • Vendor Documents Library — structured library with metadata columns, auto-tagged via Power Automate.
  • Approval Queue List — tracked multi-tier approval status with approver level (1/2/3), decision, timestamp, and comments.
  • Incidents & Issues List — custom Power Apps form for vendor performance issues with severity classification.
Automation & Integration
  • PnP PowerShell script provisioned complete 6-tier hierarchical folder structure per vendor across 3 SharePoint libraries automatically upon new vendor record creation.
  • Power Automate flow launched a 3-level sequential approval workflow via Teams Adaptive Cards with 48-hour SLA per level and auto-escalation.
  • Scheduled flow scanned all contracts for expiration within 30/60/90 days, sending personalized alerts to responsible department managers.
  • JSON column formatting rendered visual SLA status badges (active/expiring soon/expired) directly in the list view.
⬡ Also Designed & Formally Presented As

⚡Business Rule: Auto-sets vendor status to Expiring when SLA expiry date ≤ T+90 days — enforced server-side, no Power Automate dependency.
⚡Business Rule: Approval stage gating — blocks record progression until prior stage has a completed mka_approvalrecord with Approved status.

🔒RBAC: Column-level security restricts Department Managers to their department's vendor rows — enforced natively at the Dataverse layer.
Measurable Results
  • 380+ active vendors in a single normalized system across 5 departments
  • 2,300+ hierarchical folder structures provisioned automatically — replaced a multi-day manual task per vendor
  • 100% elimination of manual contract renewal tracking via Excel
  • Zero missed SLA expiration deadlines post-deployment
  • 60% faster approval turnaround through structured Teams approval routing

Clinical Supervision & Audit
Compliance Automation

A New Leaf — Behavioral Health Division  ·  Timeline: 2022–2023 (maintained through 2026)  ·  Compliance: State behavioral health & child welfare regulations
Business Problem

Clinical staff were required to complete monthly supervision documentation for child welfare cases — a regulatory requirement with zero tolerance for missing records. The process required data entry by clinical staff, sequential approvals from Clinical Directors and up to 10 concurrent supervisors, PDF dossier generation, and archival. Everything was done manually via email and physical forms, creating compliance risk and consuming significant staff time.

Microsoft Lists & Forms Architecture
  • Clinical Supervision Sessions List — 30+ columns tracking session date, supervisor, supervisee, focus area, hours, notes. Custom Power Apps form with conditional visibility for supervision types (individual/group/peer).
  • Supervisees Registry List — tracking certification requirements and compliance status. Auto-calculated compliance percentage displayed in form header.
  • Supervision Plans List — individual plans per clinician with auto-population from supervisee profile and plan status tracking.
  • Audit Evidence List — capturing audit artifacts with document links, approval signatures, and regulatory category codes. Read-only Power Apps view for auditor access.
Automation Architecture
  • Concurrent approval branches sent simultaneous requests to up to 10 supervisors; Power Automate collected all responses and computed approval quorum before proceeding.
  • Upon full approval, automated pipeline generated a structured e-signed PDF dossier merging all session data, supervisor notes, and approval timestamps.
  • PDF was encrypted, watermarked with session metadata, and automatically archived to the SharePoint Compliance Library with proper metadata tags for regulatory retrieval.
  • Monthly scheduled flow generated compliance dashboard snapshot for Clinical Directors showing completion rates by supervisee and flagging overdue sessions.
Measurable Results
  • 100% elimination of manual PDF compilation for clinical audit dossiers
  • Estimated 80% reduction in audit documentation time per supervisor
  • Zero compliance incidents related to missing documentation post-deployment
  • Full digital audit trail for every supervision session with tamper-evident PDF records

Enterprise Facilities Audit
& Compliance Engine

A New Leaf — Facilities Management Division  ·  Timeline: 2023–2024  ·  Impact: 60% reduction in audit cycle time
Business Problem

Monthly facility compliance audits required inspectors to complete detailed checklists across multiple facilities, compile inspection data into formatted PDF reports, route them through management review, and archive approved reports by deadline. The entire process was manual, taking multiple staff-days per cycle and regularly running close to regulatory deadlines.

Lists & Forms Architecture
  • Facility Registry List — master list of all facilities with address, responsible manager, inspection frequency, and compliance category. Linked to all inspection records via lookup.
  • Inspection Checklist Items List — normalized list of all audit checklist items organized by category and facility type. Used as reference lookup to build dynamic inspection forms.
  • Inspection Records List — primary capture list with custom Power Apps form providing a structured, mobile-friendly inspection experience with section-by-section navigation.
  • Deficiencies Tracking List — tracking each identified deficiency with severity, assigned responsible party, corrective action, and resolution deadline.
  • Microsoft Forms Integration — for field inspectors without Power Apps access, Microsoft Forms survey captured data automatically parsed into Inspection Records via Power Automate.
Automation Architecture
  • Power Automate parsed complex nested JSON payload from Microsoft Forms, mapping all fields to corresponding Inspection Records List columns.
  • JSON parsing extracted array-structure checklist scores, computed section totals and overall compliance percentage, and wrote calculated values back to the list.
  • Dynamic PDF generation pipeline rendered a formatted compliance report template populated with all inspection data, deficiency details, and photographic evidence.
  • Power BI report connected to Inspection Records List provided real-time compliance dashboard visible to Facilities Director.
⬡ Also Designed & Formally Presented As
Dataverse Relational Architecture ✓ Presented
This solution was formally re-presented with a complete Microsoft Dataverse data model — showing how the Microsoft Forms + SharePoint JSON parsing pipeline maps to structured Dataverse entities with referential integrity, weighted scoring Business Rules, and Power BI DirectQuery for real-time compliance dashboards.
mka_facility
Core entity. Type OptionSet, ComplianceCategory OptionSet, Manager Lookup → systemuser.
mka_inspectionrecord
1:N ← facility. OverallScore (Decimal), Status OptionSet, Approver Lookup → systemuser.
mka_checklistitem
Reusable library of 80+ items. Weight (Decimal), IsFailureCritical (Boolean), Category OptionSet.
mka_inspectionresponse
Junction: inspection × checklist item. Score per answer (Decimal). Drives weighted aggregate.
mka_deficiency
1:N ← inspection + facility. Severity OptionSet, corrective action, resolution deadline.
⚡Business Rule: Calculates mka_overallscore as weighted average of all mka_inspectionresponse.mka_score values — replaces JSON parsing flow with server-side calculation.
⚡Business Rule (Critical Failure Lock): Any response with mka_isfailurecritical = true forces inspection status to Non-Compliant regardless of overall score.
🔌C# Plugin (Post-Approval on mka_inspectionrecord): Triggers PDF generation flow and archives report to SharePoint Compliance Library with metadata — same pipeline, plugin-fired.
📊Power BI DirectQuery on Dataverse (vs. SharePoint refresh) — provides true real-time compliance dashboard. Score aggregations pushed down to Dataverse query engine.
Measurable Results
  • 60% reduction in end-to-end facility audit and approval cycle time
  • 100% elimination of manual data re-entry from paper forms to digital records
  • Zero missed monthly regulatory compliance deadlines post-deployment
  • Complete photographic evidence archive linked to every inspection record

HR Certification Dynamic Rules
& Tracking Engine

A New Leaf — Human Resources Division  ·  Timeline: 2023  ·  Scale: Organization-wide staff certification management
Business Problem

HR was required to track mandatory certification completion across all staff, assign follow-up training modules based on assessment scores, and maintain documentation for regulatory review. The process relied on manual review of assessment results, manual calculation of required follow-up modules, and manual dispatch of assignment emails — consuming significant HR staff time and creating inconsistencies.

Lists Architecture & Power Apps Forms
  • Staff Certifications Master List — central tracking with auto-calculated compliance percentage and color-coded status indicator.
  • Certification Catalog List — reference list organized by role, department, and frequency. Used as lookup source to validate assignment completeness per staff member.
  • Assessment Results List — raw assessment data from Microsoft Forms. Each submission auto-created a linked record in Staff Certifications Master List.
  • Follow-up Modules Assignment List — dynamically populated by the rules engine based on assessment scores. Custom Power Apps form for HR to review and override assignments.
  • Compliance Status Dashboard List — aggregated list updated nightly by Power Automate, connected to Power BI for executive reporting.
Dynamic Rules Engine Architecture
  • Power Automate flow parsed multi-dimensional JSON array of assessment scores, evaluating each section against a configurable threshold matrix stored in SharePoint — not hardcoded — HR could update thresholds without developer involvement.
  • Rules engine applied conditional logic to determine which follow-up modules were required: score <70% on Section 3 → assign Module 3A and 3B; score <50% on Section 1 → reassign full certification.
  • Assigned modules dispatched to staff via personalized Teams notification with direct link to assigned module.
  • Completion status updates fed back from Relias Learning (external LMS) via scheduled data reconciliation flow.
Measurable Results
  • 100% elimination of manual HR assessment review and module assignment
  • Estimated 80%+ reduction in HR administrative time per certification cycle
  • Complete auditable history for every certification event with timestamps and assigned modules
  • HR managers able to update scoring thresholds independently without IT involvement

Business Problem

A B2B business needed an integrated, mobile-first inventory and order management system supporting field sales representatives, administrative management, automated transaction processing, real-time inventory updates, and AI-assisted data querying — all within the Microsoft ecosystem without external platforms.

Solution Architecture

  • Model-Driven App for administrative management — back-office staff managed products, customers, pricing rules, and order fulfillment with Dataverse-backed custom views, dashboards, and business process flows.
  • Dataverse schema with 7 interrelated tables: Products, Customers, Orders, Order Lines, Inventory Transactions, Pricing Rules, and User Profiles. Polymorphic lookup relationships for flexible customer-product-pricing associations.

  • RBAC security model with three roles (Field Sales Rep, Admin, Manager). Field Sales Reps could only see their own customers and orders.
  • Copilot Studio Agentic AI with RAG-pattern knowledge grounding — non-technical business owners queried inventory, order status, and customer history using natural language via Teams.
  • CI/CD pipeline via Azure DevOps and Power Platform Build Tools with managed/unmanaged solution packaging across Dev/Test/Prod environments.
  • C# server-side plugin enforcing business rule: no order submission if customer account balance exceeded credit limit — validated synchronously at the Dataverse layer.
Measurable Results

AI-Powered Internal Request Automation

Architecture Design  ·  Copilot Studio · Power Automate · Dataverse · Azure OpenAI · RAG  ·  View Interactive Showcase →
Overview

End-to-end pipeline architecture designed to automate 800–1,000 daily internal employee requests while maintaining SOX compliance, full audit traceability, and risk-based human oversight. The key architectural decision: rules run before AI — deterministic filtering resolves obvious requests at zero LLM cost; the AI layer only activates for ambiguous cases.

10-Step Pipeline Architecture
  • Request Channels → Dataverse — All channels (Teams, email, intranet, service desk) write to a single Dataverse table. Audit trail begins at receipt, before any processing — SOX foundation.
  • Security Gate (Entra ID / RBAC) — Identity verified before anything moves. RAG searches later respect individual permission levels — security designed in, not bolted on.
  • Fast Path Rules Engine (Power Automate) — Regex, keyword matching, and business rules resolve password resets, VPN requests, and equipment orders in milliseconds. Zero LLM cost for the majority of volume.
  • LLM Analysis (Copilot Studio) — Only activates on no-match. Extracts intent, entities, priority, and risk level. Outputs confidence signals that feed the decision engine.
  • Decision Engine — Weighted score (intent 40%, retrieval 30%, historical 20%, risk 10%) routes to one of three paths. Risk always overrides — sensitive categories go to human regardless of score.
  • Three Routing Paths — ≥80%: auto-resolve (Action or RAG); 50–80%: AI draft + human review; <50%: direct escalation. Nothing is resolved without an appropriate level of human oversight.
Key Design Decisions
  • Rules before AI — Power Automate runs first; LLM only activates when deterministic rules cannot match
  • RAG over fine-tuning — Policies change constantly; RAG answers from live documents with citable sources for SOX audits
  • Risk always overrides — Sensitive categories force human approval regardless of confidence score
  • Microsoft native stack — Governance, identity, and audit built in; no security layer to build from scratch
  • Reusable pipeline pattern — Not a one-time bot; every future agent uses the same security gate, audit trail, and confidence routing

Document Automation — Scan to Dataverse

Production System  ·  Power Automate · AI Builder · Dataverse · SharePoint  ·  View Full Case Study →
Overview

Built a production scan-to-Dataverse pipeline that takes a physical document off a scanner and creates a structured, queryable Dataverse record — automatically, in under 30 seconds. Zero manual touch points. Zero custom code. Staff place a document in the scanner and walk away.

7-Step Pipeline
  • Scanner → Shared Mailbox — MFP scanner emails the scanned document (PDF/image) to a dedicated M365 shared mailbox. The only human action in the entire pipeline.
  • Power Automate Trigger — Flow fires the instant an email with attachment arrives. No polling, no batch — real-time.
  • AI Builder Document Processing — The intelligence layer. Custom-trained model reads the PDF and extracts Invoice Number, Date, Vendor Name, Total Amount, Due Date — with confidence scores per field.
  • Dataverse Record Created — Extracted fields map directly to Dataverse table columns. Structured, searchable, reportable data — not just a stored file.
  • SharePoint Storage — Original scanned file saved to SharePoint document library as source of truth, linked to the Dataverse record by unique ID.
  • Post-Processing — Email archived, staff notified only if confidence score falls below threshold. Otherwise completes silently.
V1 → V2 Evolution

Version 1 (production) solved routing and storage — scan goes to SharePoint, list record created, staff notified. The gap: document content still required manual review. Version 2 adds AI Builder — same trigger, same flow architecture, dramatically different data quality. The extraction problem is eliminated entirely.

Production Results
  • 0 manual steps between scanner and Dataverse record
  • Under 30 seconds end-to-end processing per document
  • Zero transcription errors — AI Builder handles extraction
  • Fully searchable records — invoice number, vendor, amount queryable in Dataverse
  • 100% audit trail — timestamp, source email, confidence scores logged
  • Infinitely scalable — same pipeline handles 1 or 1,000 docs/day
