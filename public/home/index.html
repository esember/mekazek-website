Services

Solutions shaped around your needs

From a single approval flow to company-wide multi-agent systems — we design, build, and maintain automation at every scale.

01 — Process Automation

Make manual work invisible

Moving data between spreadsheets, chasing approvals over email, copy-pasting across systems… We automate every repetitive task that eats your team's day using Power Automate, n8n, and RPA tools.

  • Approval and request flows (procurement, leave, expenses)
  • Document processing: invoices, contracts, forms (AI-powered OCR)
  • Cross-system data synchronization (ERP ↔ CRM ↔ M365)
  • Scheduled reporting and notification flows
Documents come in, automation does the work, verified records come out
02 — Agentic AI

Set the goal, let the agents handle it

Classic automation runs on rules; agentic AI runs on goals. We design systems of AI agents that gather data, analyze it, make decisions, and hand work to each other — with the human-approval checkpoints defined together with you.

  • Multi-agent architecture design and orchestration
  • Assistants grounded on your company data (RAG)
  • Autonomous reporting, analysis, and research agents
  • Safe human-in-the-loop flows
The orchestrator distributes tasks to specialist agents and merges the results
03 — Copilot Studio Agents

Your company's 24/7 assistant

With Microsoft Copilot Studio we build assistants that are grounded on your knowledge base and can actually take action. Your people ask questions, open tickets, and find information without leaving Teams — while HR and IT focus only on the work that truly needs a human.

  • HR assistant: leave, payroll, policy questions
  • IT help desk: ticket creation, passwords, setup guides
  • Sales assistant: quotes, product and stock info
  • Rollout to Teams, web, and mobile channels

About

We translate technology into your business

Mekazek Studio is a boutique consulting studio that moves internal processes into automation. Our goal is not to sell software — it is to build systems that give teams their time back and produce measurable results.

Why Mekazek Studio?

Synapses connect the neurons in your brain; we connect the systems, teams, and data in your company. On every project we focus on understanding the process first — and choose the technology second.

Mekazek Studio was founded by Gursel Olca — a US-based Senior Power Platform Developer with a computer science degree and a master's in machine learning. Across a 19+ year career in IT, data analytics, and automation, he has shipped 200+ production applications and workflows for organizations ranging from government institutions to multi-department enterprises.

Deep expertise in the Microsoft Power Platform, Dataverse, and Copilot Studio ecosystem; broad experience with Python, LangChain, and modern web technologies (React, TypeScript, SPFx) — working at every scale.
